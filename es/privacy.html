Privacy / prywatność

Guía de la versión 1.0.1 (compilación 4), comprobada en el código iOS el 17 de noviembre de 2026.

Recording and analysis are local. The app does not send recordings, transcripts, patient records or voice profiles to the developer or a cloud transcription service.

Models are downloaded from FluidInference on Hugging Face when needed; App Store is used for the one-time purchase and restore. These services receive technical connection data, not session content.

Recordings and transcripts are excluded from system backup by the code. Patient records, settings and voice profiles in preferences are not explicitly excluded and may be included in device or iCloud backup according to iOS settings.

Exports are created only by your action and are not encrypted by the app. Sharing to iCloud Drive, email or another service may transfer selected data outside the device. Deleting app data does not delete prior exports or backups.

Use a device passcode, keep access to backups restricted, verify recipients before sharing, and follow the legal and professional rules applicable to your practice.
